# PAIR Scholarly Reflection

The PAIR Scholarly Reflection is the weekly critical-reading framework used in ANLY 735 – Research Seminar in Predictive AI.

PAIR stands for:

- **P — Position:** What is the paper trying to establish?
- **A — Analyze:** How convincing is the evidence?
- **I — Interrogate:** What should a critical data scientist question?
- **R — Reimagine:** How would you extend or improve the research?

## Purpose

The goal is to critically evaluate Predictive AI research from the perspective of a developing data science researcher and not just summarize the assigned paper.

A strong PAIR reflection demonstrates that you can:

1. identify the scholarly contribution;
2. evaluate the evidence and methodology;
3. question assumptions, limitations, and implications; and
4. generate a meaningful research extension.

## Recommended Length

Approximately **700–900 words**, excluding references.

Quality of analysis is more important than length.

## Submission Workflow

1. Clone or download the template.
2. Add the weekly paper to Zotero.
3. Add the citation to `references.bib`.
4. Complete the reflection in `pair-reflection.qmd`.
5. Render the document to Microsoft Word.
6. Review the rendered document.
7. Commit and push your source files to GitHub.
8. Submit the `.docx` file to Canvas.

## Important

PAIR is a scholarly reflection, not a traditional article summary.

**Describe less. Analyze more.**
